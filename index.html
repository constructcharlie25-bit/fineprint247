Questions

The short answers.

Why not just paste my contract into ChatGPT?

A general chatbot gives you a summary and hopes you asked the right follow-ups. FinePrint gives you a 0–100 risk score, severity-tagged clause findings, and per-clause "why it matters" and "what to negotiate" guidance — with zero prompt engineering, no account, no $20/mo subscription, and your contract is never used to train models. See the full comparison.

Is this legal advice?

No. FinePrint spots common contractual risks and explains them in plain English, but it is not a law firm and nothing here is legal advice. For high-stakes agreements, talk to a licensed attorney.

What kinds of contracts can I scan?

Client service agreements, MSAs, SOWs, contractor agreements, NDAs — anything written. Paste the text or upload a PDF or DOCX.

Who is it for?

Freelancers, independent contractors, and small agency owners who regularly sign client paperwork without a lawyer on call.

Is my contract kept private?

Your text is sent securely for analysis and is never sold or shared. We don't train models on your contracts.

What does the report include?

An overall risk score (0–100), a plain-English summary, and every flagged clause with its risk level, why it matters, and what to negotiate.

Can I try it free?

Yes — hit "Scan a contract" and try the built-in demo on a sample agreement. No signup, no credit card.
